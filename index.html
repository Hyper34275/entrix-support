Frequently asked questions

Quick fixes

My computer will not pair with the app
  1. Confirm the iPhone or iPad and Windows computer can reach the internet.
  2. Generate a fresh pairing QR code in Entrix for Windows; pairing codes expire.
  3. Scan the 2FA QR first, then scan the iPhone/iPad Pair QR when Windows asks for it.
  4. If the computer belongs to another Apple account, remove it from that account before pairing again.
A Windows login request does not appear
  1. Open Entrix and confirm Premium is active under Settings.
  2. Allow notifications for Entrix in iOS or iPadOS Settings.
  3. Confirm the computer is online and still paired to the same Apple account.
  4. Retry from Windows; approval requests are intentionally short-lived.
Premium is missing after purchase

Sign in with the same Apple Account used for the purchase, open the Premium screen, and select Restore Purchases. Also verify that the device is online and the subscription is active in Apple subscription settings.

How do I delete my Entrix account?

Open Entrix → Settings → Account → Delete Entrix Account. The app asks you to verify with Apple before cloud data and local Entrix secrets are removed. Deleting the app alone does not cancel an Apple subscription.
