Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Entrix handles information when you use the Entrix app for iPhone or iPad, the Windows companion, and related cloud services.

Effective and last updated: August 7, 2026

On this page Information we handle How we use information Storage and security Service providers Retention and deletion Your choices Contact

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to Entrix features that pair an Apple device with a Windows computer, provide time-based one-time passwords (2FA), deliver security alerts, approve sign-in requests, perform remote computer commands, and manage an Entrix Premium entitlement.

2. Information we handle

Account information

Entrix requires Sign in with Apple. We receive an account identifier used to authenticate you. Apple may also provide your name or relay email address when you first authorize the app, depending on the choices you make with Apple.

Paired-computer information

We process identifiers and display names for paired computers, platform and app-version information, pairing state, public or derived security material needed to establish the device relationship, and recent connection status.

Security and command activity

Entrix processes security-alert details, login-approval requests and decisions, remote command type and status, associated computer identifiers, and timestamps. This information is necessary to deliver the requested security feature and show its result.

Purchases and entitlement

Apple processes subscription payment details. Entrix receives and verifies StoreKit transaction and entitlement information, such as product, subscription state, and validity dates. Entrix does not receive your full payment-card number.

Notifications and diagnostics

We process an Apple/Firebase push-notification token, its production or sandbox environment, and delivery state. Firebase App Check may process device and app-integrity signals to protect the service from abuse. Standard server logs may include request timing, error details, and network metadata such as an IP address.

Secrets stored on your device

Entrix stores paired-device keys, 2FA secrets, and recovery codes in the Apple Keychain on your device. Encrypted synchronization payloads may pass through the cloud as part of the Windows-to-device sync flow, but Entrix is designed so sensitive values are protected in transit and at rest on the device.

3. How we use information

  • Authenticate your account and maintain the secure pairing with your computers.
  • Generate and display 2FA codes and synchronized recovery information.
  • Deliver failed-login alerts and short-lived login-approval requests.
  • Send remote lock, restart, or shutdown commands you request.
  • Verify Premium purchases, restore entitlements, and prevent purchase fraud.
  • Protect Entrix, investigate errors, prevent abuse, and maintain service reliability.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce applicable terms.

We do not sell personal information and do not use Entrix account data for third-party advertising.

4. Storage and security

Entrix uses technical safeguards including encrypted HTTPS connections, authenticated cloud requests, App Check, hashed server-side machine credentials, limited-use tokens for sensitive deletion operations, and Apple Keychain protection for local secrets. No storage or transmission system can guarantee absolute security.

Do not send passwords, 2FA secrets, QR pairing codes, recovery codes, access tokens, or other confidential values through a public support issue.

5. Service providers

Entrix relies on service providers only where needed to operate the app:

  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, StoreKit subscriptions, Keychain, biometrics, and Apple Push Notification service.
  • Google Firebase — authentication, databases, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging, and App Check.
  • GitHub Pages — hosting this public support and privacy website. GitHub may receive ordinary web-request information when you visit it.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Their systems may process data in countries other than your own.

6. Retention and deletion

We retain account, entitlement, and paired-computer records while needed to operate your Entrix account. Short-lived approval requests and operational records are retained only as needed for delivery, security, troubleshooting, and abuse prevention. Some security and command records may be retained for a limited period after completion.

You can permanently delete your Entrix account from Entrix → Settings → Account → Delete Entrix Account. The deletion flow verifies your Apple identity, removes Entrix cloud account data and paired-machine ownership, and then clears local Entrix secrets after the server confirms deletion.

Deleting Entrix does not automatically cancel an Apple subscription. Subscription management remains available in Apple account settings.

7. Your choices

  • Control notification permissions in iOS or iPadOS Settings.
  • Remove an individual paired computer from Entrix Settings.
  • Delete your entire Entrix account using the in-app deletion flow.
  • Manage or cancel Premium through Apple subscription settings.
  • Revoke Sign in with Apple access through your Apple Account settings.

8. Children

Entrix is a computer-security utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Entrix changes. We will post the revised policy at this URL and update the effective date above. Material changes may also be communicated in the app where appropriate.

10. Contact

For a general privacy or support question, open an issue in the Entrix Support repository. Because issues are public, do not include personal or sensitive information. For account deletion, use the private in-app deletion flow described above.
